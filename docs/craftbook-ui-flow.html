Craftbook
orders that count their own materials.

Twenty screens, in the order you'd actually touch them: an order comes in from Shopee, the app works out which magnet sheets and photo tops it will eat, holds them, deducts them when you pack, and tells you what to rebuy before a product goes dark.

PACK OF 20 · 6 LEFT

The pip strip is the one visual idea in the app. You buy in packs, so stock is drawn as the pack: one mark per piece, filled = still on the shelf. Hatched marks are pieces already promised to open orders.

How the whole thing hangs together
  • Products hold a recipe of materials. Materials are stored in pieces, bought in packs, and carry a weighted average unit cost.
  • Saving an order reserves pieces. Packing an order deducts them. Nothing moves in between, so a cancelled order costs you nothing.
  • Materials used lives on the order and is editable, so waste is recorded where it happens and profit stays true.
  • Alert levels are per material; the buy list reads the recipes backwards to name the products and orders each shortage blocks.
  • No accounts, no sync, no network calls. Backup is a file you export yourself.
